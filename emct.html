🚀 14 AHT Reduction Techniques from the Guide

🔗 Technique #1: Combine Questions

PHASE 1: OPENING
❌ DON'T SAY:
"May I have your name?"
[wait]
"Thank you. And which location would you like to pick up from?"
[wait]
"Great. And are you flying in or are you a local renter?"
Time wasted: 45 seconds
✅ DO SAY:
"May I have your name and pickup location? And are you flying in or local?"
⏱️ Time saved: 20 seconds

📦 Technique #2: Bundle Essential Questions

PHASE 2: NEEDS ASSESSMENT
❌ DON'T ASK ONE AT A TIME:
"What's your pickup date?"
"What time?"
"What's your return date?"
"What time will you return?"
"Same location?"
Time wasted: 60 seconds
✅ DO SAY:
"What are your pickup and return dates and times? And will you return to the same location?"
⏱️ Time saved: 30 seconds

🎯 Technique #3: Strategic Questions = Efficiency

PHASE 2: NEEDS ASSESSMENT
❌ ASK SEPARATELY:
"Where are you going?"
"Do you need GPS?"
"Do you need PlatePass?"
✅ ASK ONCE, UNLOCK MULTIPLE ADD-ONS:
"Where are you headed, and are you familiar with the area?"

This ONE question tells you:
- Destination (PlatePass opportunity)
- Familiarity (GPS opportunity)
- Trip type (Protection needs)
⏱️ Time saved: 45 seconds

⌨️ Technique #4: Listen & Type Simultaneously

EFFICIENCY TIP
⚡ EFFICIENCY TIP:
✓ Type customer information WHILE they're speaking
✓ Don't ask them to repeat unless absolutely necessary
✓ Use shorthand in notes, clean up during after-call work
⏱️ Time saved: 30–60 seconds per call

📋 Technique #5: Batch Requirements

PHASE 3: REQUIREMENTS & AGE
❌ DON'T SAY SEPARATELY:
"You'll need a credit card."
"You'll also need a driver's license."
"The license needs to be in your name."
"The credit card also needs to be in your name."
"You'll need liability coverage..."
Time wasted: 50 seconds
✅ DO SAY:
"Quick requirements while I check availability: You'll need a credit card and driver's license in your name, plus state-mandated liability coverage. Are you over 25?"
⏱️ Time saved: 25 seconds

🗺️ Technique #6: Nevada Exception - Only When Relevant

PHASE 3: REQUIREMENTS & AGE
❌ DON'T:
Explain Nevada requirements to EVERY customer
✅ DO:
Only mention if customer is actually renting in Nevada

"Since you're renting in Nevada, you'll need to show proof of liability coverage at pickup—physical card or digital copy both work."
⏱️ Time saved: 15 seconds per non-Nevada call

💰 Technique #7: Prepaid First, Always (No Comparison)

PHASE 4: RATE PRESENTATION
❌ DON'T SAY:
"We have two options for you today. We have Pay Now at $350 and Pay Later at $425. Pay Now gives you the lowest rate and locks in your reservation, but the link expires in 30 minutes. Pay Later gives you flexibility but costs more. Which would you prefer?"
Time wasted: 30 seconds + decision paralysis
✅ DO SAY:
"Great! I have a [vehicle] available. Our Prepaid rate is $350—that's our lowest rate and it reserves your car. Most customers choose this. Let me go ahead and reserve this for you?"
⏱️ Time saved: 20 seconds + increases conversion

🎯 Technique #8: Assumptive Language = Faster Close

PHASE 4: RATE PRESENTATION
❌ ASKING CREATES DELAYS:
"Would you like to book this?"
"Does that work for you?"
"What do you think?"
"Are you interested?"
✅ ASSUMING MOVES FORWARD:
"Let me lock that in for you"
"I'll go ahead and reserve this"
"Perfect, let me get this booked"
"What email should I send the confirmation to?"
⏱️ Time saved: 15–30 seconds

🧩 Technique #9: Integrate Add-Ons During Detail Gathering

PHASE 5: ADD-ONS
❌ DON'T:
Finish all details, THEN go back and offer add-ons separately
✅ DO:
Weave add-ons into the conversation naturally

Example: "May I have your email address?"
[Customer provides email]
"Perfect. And since you mentioned you're heading to Orlando with the kids, I'll include our PlatePass for the toll roads—just $13.99 total—and a child safety seat. Sound good?"
"Great. And your contact number?"
⏱️ Time saved: 45 seconds

📦 Technique #10: Bundle Add-Ons (Package Approach)

PHASE 5: ADD-ONS
❌ DON'T OFFER ONE AT A TIME:
"Would you like LDW?" → explain → wait
"How about GPS?" → explain → wait
"Do you want PlatePass?" → explain → wait
Time wasted: 90+ seconds
✅ DO BUNDLE:
"For your Florida trip with the family, I'll include our Protection Package with LDW, PlatePass for tolls, and GPS—that's $XX per day and covers everything you need. Sound good?"
⏱️ Time saved: 60–90 seconds

💡 Technique #11: Recommend, Don't Ask

PHASE 5: ADD-ONS
❌ ASKING = LONGER CONVERSATIONS:
"We have Loss Damage Waiver available. It covers damage to the vehicle if something happens. It's optional. Would you like to add it?"
[Customer: "What does it cover exactly?"]
[You explain again]
[Customer: "How much is it?"]
Time wasted: 90+ seconds
✅ RECOMMENDING = FASTER ACCEPTANCE:
"I'm including our Loss Damage Waiver—it protects you from any damage costs. That's $29 per day."
[Customer usually accepts or declines quickly]
⏱️ Time saved: 60 seconds

📝 Technique #12: Batch Information Requests

PHASE 6: DETAIL GATHERING
❌ DON'T ASK SEPARATELY:
"What's your email?"
[wait]
"And your phone number?"
[wait]
"And your zip code?"
Time wasted: 30 seconds
✅ DO SAY:
"I'll need your email, contact number, and zip code to complete the booking."
[Customer provides all at once]
⏱️ Time saved: 20 seconds

✅ Technique #13: Streamlined Recap

PHASE 7: RECAP & CLOSE
❌ DON'T OVER-EXPLAIN:
"Okay, so just to confirm, you're picking up from Orlando International Airport, which is located at 1 Jeff Fuqua Boulevard, Orlando, Florida 32827, on June 15th, 2026 at 3:00 PM Eastern Standard Time, and you'll be returning the vehicle to the exact same location, Orlando International Airport, on June 20th, 2026 at 3:00 PM Eastern Standard Time..."
Time wasted: 45 seconds
✅ DO SAY:
"Perfect! Confirming: Orlando Airport pickup June 15th at 3 PM, return June 20th at 3 PM, same location. Ford Escape or similar, total $487.50 including taxes and add-ons. Correct?"
⏱️ Time saved: 30 seconds

🔐 Technique #14: Efficient Confirmation

PHASE 7: RECAP & CLOSE
❌ DON'T:
"Your confirmation number is F-O-X-1-2-3-4-5-6-7-8. Let me repeat that: F as in Frank, O as in Oscar, X as in X-ray, 1-2-3-4-5-6-7-8. Did you get that? Would you like me to repeat it again?"
Time wasted: 45 seconds
✅ DO:
"Your confirmation number is FOX12345678—I'm sending it to your email now along with full details."
⏱️ Time saved: 25 seconds
🚫 Top 10 Time-Wasters to Avoid

1️⃣ Over-Explaining Simple Concepts

❌ "Unlimited mileage means you can drive as many miles as you want, there's no limit, you won't be charged per mile, you can go anywhere..."
✅ "This includes unlimited mileage."
⏱️ Time saved: 15 seconds

2️⃣ Unnecessary Small Talk

❌ "Oh, you're going to Disney World? That's so fun! I went there last year with my family. The weather should be great this time of year. Have you been before? You're going to love it!"
✅ "Perfect! For your Disney trip, I'll include PlatePass and GPS."
⏱️ Time saved: 30 seconds

3️⃣ Repeating Information Already Provided

❌ Customer says: "I'm picking up June 15th at 3 PM"
You say: "Okay, so June 15th. And what time? Oh, 3 PM, got it."
✅ "Got it—June 15th at 3 PM." [move on]
⏱️ Time saved: 10 seconds

4️⃣ Asking for Confirmation on Every Detail

❌ "Is that spelled J-O-H-N?" "And your last name is Smith, S-M-I-T-H?" "Your email is john@email.com, that's J-O-H-N at E-M-A-I-L dot com?"
✅ Only confirm unusual spellings or unclear audio
⏱️ Time saved: 30 seconds

5️⃣ Long Holds Without Preparation

❌ "Let me put you on hold while I check availability..." [120-second hold while you search]
✅ Have system ready, search while asking questions
⏱️ Time saved: 60–90 seconds

6️⃣ Explaining Every Possible Scenario

❌ "If you're late, you might lose the reservation, but if you call us, we might be able to hold it, unless it's a busy day, then we might not have availability, but we'll try our best..."
✅ "If your plans change, call us and we'll assist you."
⏱️ Time saved: 20 seconds

7️⃣ Offering Declined Add-Ons Multiple Times

❌ Customer declines GPS
You: "Are you sure? It's really helpful..."
Customer: "No thanks"
You: "It's only $X per day..."
Customer: "I'm good"
You: "Okay, but if you change your mind..."
✅ Offer once, attempt one rebuttal, move on
⏱️ Time saved: 45 seconds

8️⃣ Reading Entire Terms & Conditions

❌ Reading full T&Cs verbally during the call
✅ "Full terms are in the email I'm sending. Key points: bring your credit card and license at pickup."
⏱️ Time saved: 60+ seconds

9️⃣ Apologizing Excessively

❌ "I'm so sorry for the wait. I apologize for taking so long. Sorry about that. I'm really sorry..."
✅ "Thank you for your patience." [once, then move on]
⏱️ Time saved: 15 seconds

🔟 Asking "Anything Else?" Multiple Times

❌ At the end of every phase: "Is there anything else I can help you with?"
✅ Ask ONCE at the very end of the call
⏱️ Time saved: 30 seconds
🎓 How to Coach AHT Reduction
1
Identify the Root Cause
Listen to calls and categorize time usage:

☐ Excessive small talk
☐ Over-explaining concepts
☐ Asking questions one at a time
☐ Long holds/system delays
☐ Repeating information unnecessarily
☐ Offering declined add-ons multiple times
☐ Inefficient objection handling
☐ Lack of call structure
☐ Typing slowly/system navigation issues
☐ Uncertain/hesitant language
2
Use Side-by-Side Comparison
Show agent TWO versions of the same interaction to highlight the difference between inefficient and efficient approaches. This visual comparison helps agents understand exactly what needs to change.
3
The "60-Second Challenge"
Coaching Exercise: "Let's see if you can complete the Needs Assessment phase in 60 seconds or less."

Agent practices:
- Bundling questions
- Typing while listening
- Moving quickly but naturally

Debrief: What worked? What felt rushed? How can we balance speed and quality?
4
Celebrate Efficiency Wins
Recognition Program:
- Highlight agents who improve AHT while maintaining quality
- Share best practices with the team
- Create friendly competition with weekly efficiency champions

📋 What is SMART Coaching?
S – Specific: Clearly define what needs to be achieved
M – Measurable: Include metrics to track progress
A – Achievable: Realistic given available resources and time
R – Relevant: Aligned with overall performance goals
T – Time-bound: Has a clear deadline or timeframe
